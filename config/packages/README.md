Live collection of plug-and-play Home Assistant packages. Each YAML file in this folder bundles sensors, helpers, automations, scripts, and Lovelace assets needed for a specific subsystem so you can drop it into `configuration.yaml` with a single `packages:` include.

![Home Assistant header](https://i.imgur.com/vjDH1LJ.png)

### Why packages exist
- Keep everything for a subsystem (alarm, garage, water shutoff, etc.) in one file, which makes versioning, troubleshooting, and sharing dramatically easier.
- Encapsulate helpers (input_booleans, sensors, scripts) so enabling/disabling a capability is as simple as moving one file in or out of the `packages/` include.
- Document hardware dependencies right next to the logic so you remember which gear keeps each automation alive.

### How to adapt a package quickly
- Enable `packages:` under `homeassistant:` in your `configuration.yaml`, then drop any of these YAML files into the folder with your own entity IDs/secrets.
- Scan the comments at the top of each package for credential requirements, MQTT topics, or REST endpoints that need to be swapped.
- Need reusable triggers or button automations? Check the companion [triggers](triggers) subfolder for examples before rolling your own.

Kitchen Show note: Native entities use canonical unsuffixed IDs; retired MQTT duplicates and their obsolete history have been removed. `kiosk_tablet.yaml` uses Kiosk Satellite's native ESPHome entity set for display control, health, screenshots, and updates. The policy keeps the panel reachable and sets master volume to 13% while either bed is occupied and restores 75% when both are empty, with reconciliation at 08:00 and startup; while `group.bed` is on or the family is away, the Dim screensaver goes dark instead of using unreliable true panel-off. A Frigate Driveway alert wakes the household kiosks and holds the live Driveway feed until occupancy clears or the ten-minute safety limit expires, below Garage and Front Door in camera priority.

After Home Assistant starts, each enrolled native Show waits three minutes for the initial state burst to settle, reloads its dashboard page once if the device is reachable, then captures a fresh screenshot. Periodic screenshot refreshes do not reload the page.

Kitchen, Upstairs and Office voice use Kiosk Satellite's [native Assist satellite](https://kiosksatellite.com/docs/voice-satellite/) through the existing ESPHome connection. The voice overlay uses Docked mode with a light card at bottom center, keeping the dashboard visible during conversations. The Kitchen Joanna pipeline uses a dedicated GPT-5.6 Luna conversation agent with reasoning off and standard Assist controls, tries local intents first, and retains Home Assistant Cloud speech recognition and Jenny's voice. The announcement agent keeps its separate prompt and uses GPT-6.1 Terra, as does the OpenAI AI Task service. Assistant and wake-word choices are managed on the device's Home Assistant controls; wake-word detection runs on the kiosk, while command recognition and spoken replies use the selected Assist pipeline. [Custom wake-word models](https://kiosksatellite.com/docs/custom-wake-words/) can be imported directly under Kiosk Satellite's Voice Satellite > Wake Word settings without adding dashboard resources or restarting Home Assistant.

Native Show enrollment is centralized in `satellite_devices()` in [kiosk_health.jinja](../custom_templates/kiosk_health.jinja). Each entry supplies its stable maintenance key, native node, entity suffix, dashboard path, volume-policy opt-in, and local idle-sleep exception. The daily 07:50 app-update pass processes followers before the fleet leader, stages a signed Kiosk Satellite update, then checks installed version, connectivity, dashboard, remote administration, voice availability, and a fresh screenshot. A stalled installer or incomplete recovery dispatches the enrolled device to the authorized maintenance agent. The app update scope excludes Android/LineageOS and unrelated software. Upstairs and Office volumes are manual; only Kitchen opts into the bed-dependent volume policy. Screen/event policies and snapshot refresh use the same inventory.

Kitchen and Office default to [AirPlay](https://github.com/jxlarrea/kiosk-satellite/blob/main/docs/sendspin.md#airplay): source `This device`, player `AirPlay`, receiver enabled. The receiver toggle follows the fleet's Media Player profile, but source and player selection stay local to each device; Upstairs still needs its app update and local player selection. Each receiver advertises its own device name and uses the existing device volume policy. AirPlay 1 supports audio; video, screen mirroring, and AirPlay 2 multi-room groups are unsupported. Selecting AirPlay takes the local Sendspin player offline.

### Featured packages (live today)
The kiosk package also exposes `sensor.kiosk_recent_phone_notifications`: a restoring, bounded feed of shared parent/family phone pushes, including title-only notices such as Life360 arrivals. All three Shows include those messages in their existing ten-second slide rotation for five minutes; the Bedroom message band uses the same five-minute feed and alternates them with household reminders while weather, security, and water-heater safety alerts retain priority. The round Living Room Nest gives shared notices an exclusive two-minute display window, then resumes its photo gallery. Entries show their sent time, replace by notification tag, and expire automatically within fifteen seconds of their deadline. Phone notification buttons and camera attachments remain on the phones.

- Downstairs uses cooling only. From sunset until 08:00, a fresh occupied transition can start bedtime at 77F after both beds report occupied continuously for six minutes. The 80F reset runs every day at 08:00, even if the beds remain occupied. Daytime bed occupancy, leaving bed, and sensor recovery alone do not change the target. The next morning deadline, current period, manual intent, and confirmed command restore across restarts. A missed morning reset is caught on startup or the five-minute reconciliation.
- Guest Mode selects 76F for gatherings and prevents away Eco. Switching it off returns to the current day/bedtime target; each Guest Mode change clears temperature/Eco holds, while manual Off stays off. The dial can still override 76F during a gathering.
- With guest mode off, ten continuous minutes away enters Nest Eco; returning home restores the current period or a valid manual hold. Physical dial/app temperature and Eco changes hold until bedtime, the morning reset, or a Guest Mode change. Manual Off persists until the thermostat is switched back on or **Resume Downstairs HVAC Schedule** is run. Nest Auto-Schedule and its stored temperature schedule must both be disabled/cleared. The controller exits Eco before setting a cooling target and verifies reported state before accepting commands.
- After downstairs humidity stays above 60% for one hour, Home Assistant can request 75F for up to 20 minutes while someone is home and cooling is idle. The cycle ends early at 58% humidity or 75F room temperature. Starts are at least two hours apart. The timer restores across restarts, and the controller resumes the current schedule when drying ends. Manual holds, Guest Mode, away Eco, and grid and door protections take priority.
- Downstairs keeps its daytime target during hot weather. Grid-off (six minutes), grid recovery (ten minutes), entry-open (five minutes), and entry-closed (five minutes) protections remain. Upstairs retains its existing targets and triggers. Bed transitions missed entirely while HA is offline cannot be reconstructed; the restored period remains until a fresh bed transition or the morning reset.
- Upstairs reconciliation waits for a known entry-point state before restoring cooling, while grid-off shutoff remains immediate. Thermostat announcements describe mode changes rather than claiming that cooling is running.
- The main sliding door reminder can qualify while the downstairs Nest is idle or actively cooling. A parent must be home, both beds empty, the sun above the horizon and before local sunset, Guest Mode off, and the slider and monitored entries closed. Outside must be at most 76F, at least 2F cooler than downstairs, and at least 1F below the current Nest target. Outdoor dew point must be at most 60F and at least 2F below indoors, rain and weather alerts absent, rain chance at most 20%, and gusts at most 20 mph. The existing outdoor Airly sensor must report a CAQI of 0-25 (very low) within the past two hours. Missing or stale inputs suppress advice. Nest supplies indoor temperature and humidity for the dew point calculation. Pirate Weather supplies the outdoor weather readings.

A stable tag plus explicit clears replaces the previous phone notice. The persistent HA notice and the dollar-sign card in the shared kiosk rotation stay visible while useful, up to local sunset. Opening a monitored entry or losing eligible conditions clears phones, the persistent notice, and the kiosk item. Issue and clear events are recorded under CLIMATE for review. ### Dreame vacuum automations
- Logic lives in [`vacuum.yaml`](vacuum.yaml): continuous four-phase loop (sweep main, sweep baths, mop main, mop baths) driven by `input_select.l10s_vacuum_phase` and `input_text.l10s_vacuum_room_queue`, with per-room notifications and automatic reseeding between phases.
- Uses the Dreame HACS integration with segment IDs to enforce bathrooms last in each sweep/mop pass. An away departure starts or resumes the remaining queue; after a full four-phase cycle the On-Demand switch turns off and the queue resets.
- Family return stops the active task before docking, including mid-cycle mop returns. The remaining room queue is retained until the next departure; a 10-minute home trigger provides a retry if the first arrival edge was missed.
- At 5 PM, a new cycle may start only while the family is away and at least four full days after the last phase started. Vacation Mode does not disable this four-day cadence.
- Room queue advances on a 2-minute dwell in `sensor.l10s_vacuum_current_room` (queue = remaining rooms); phase changes happen when an empty queue is reseeded and the vacuum is not actively cleaning.
- One-off room cleaning for Alexa uses `input_boolean.l10s_vacuum_clean_*` (example: "Kitchen Clean") and runs a segment job without touching or checking the phased queue.
- Formal Dining (room 17/dock) is excluded from phased queues; clean it via the one-off toggle.
