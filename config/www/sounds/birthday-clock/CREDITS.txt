Birthday clock sound pack

Each hour pairs one handclap with one piano note per count, one second apart.
The piano finishes the phrase under the group greeting. At the half-hour,
play cuckoo-clock-half.wav for one solo piano note.

Piano (a sampled Steinway B) and handclaps: Versilian Community Sample Library,
Versilian Studios. https://versilian-studios.com/vcsl/
Happy Birthday Crowd, recorded by theuned: https://freesound.org/people/theuned/sounds/391869/
Both sound sources are available under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The samples were excerpted, arranged and mixed for this clock.
